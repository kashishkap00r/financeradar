# Shareability ideas (backlog)

Goal (Kashish, 14 Nov 2026): make Finance Radar spread the way Sensibull's verified P&L
cards do. Sharing should make the sharer look good, and every share should carry a
visible Finance Radar plug.

Why the Sensibull card works: (1) the sharer gains status, (2) the brand is the proof
stamp, so it can't be cropped out without losing the point, (3) the brand is inside the
image, not just in a link, (4) clicks land on the platform.

Guardrail: the About page promises no engagement bait, no tracking, no accounts. Nothing
here may break that. No streaks, leaderboards of readers, or "share to unlock".

Already shipped (13 Nov 2026): "· Share" on every item, "Share story" on Big Stories,
`utm_source=financeradar.news` on outbound links, share-preview image, SEO/AI-crawler pass.

Size: S = an afternoon, M = a day or two, L = needs a backend (D1 etc.).

## A. Proof stamp (Sensibull-style)
- [ ] 1. **"Called it" receipts.** "Finance Radar had this at 9:14 AM, 5 hours before it was everywhere." Needs first-seen timestamps per story. **M**
- [ ] 2. **"Source-checked" card.** When a news story covers an RBI/SEBI circular, the share includes "Read the original" linked to the document in From the Source. **S–M**
- [ ] 3. **Consensus stamp.** "4 of 4 AI models picked this as today's #1 story" badge on shared story cards. **S**

## B. Writers and publishers do the sharing
- [ ] 4. **"Featured on Finance Radar" badges.** Ready-made card for a Big Story, a Slow Reads pick or a top-ranked report, for the writer to post. **M**
- [ ] 5. **Monthly "most-picked writers"** (like LinkedIn Top Voice). Everyone on it shares it. **M**
- [ ] 6. **Annual "Finance Radar picks of 2026"**: best explainer, report, Slow Read. **M**

## C. Conversation-starter cards
- [ ] 7. **"Same story, 9 headlines."** How Mint / ET / Reuters framed one event, from Big Stories clusters. Unique to Finance Radar. **M**
- [ ] 8. **Bull vs bear cards** from Who Said What debate clusters. **S–M**
- [ ] 9. **Quote cards** from Who Said What, in The Chatter's visual style. **S**

## D. WhatsApp-group forwards
- [ ] 10. **"Share today's brief"** — one tap, the day's Big Stories as a WhatsApp-ready message with a branded image. **S–M** ← chosen first (14 Nov 2026)
- [ ] 11. **"This Week in Indian markets" image** every Monday from the calendar. **S–M**
- [ ] 12. **Branded screenshots.** Faint "financeradar.news" on Big Stories, quotes and the calendar, so screenshots carry the brand. **S**
- [ ] 13. **Calendar subscription plug.** Every event in `calendar.ics` says "via Finance Radar" with a link. **S**

## E. Personal and playful (lean toward "engagement", so be careful)
- [ ] 14. **Weekly quiz, Wordle-style share.** 5 questions from the week's Big Stories, "Finance Radar Quiz #12: 🟩🟩🟥🟩🟩". **M**
- [ ] 15. **"Radar Wrapped".** Year in review computed in the browser from bookmarks, no tracking. **M**
- [ ] 16. **Guess the number.** Guess CPI/GDP before release, share how close you were. **L**

## F. Pages that pull people onto Finance Radar
- [ ] 17. **A page per Big Story** with its own preview image, so shares land on Finance Radar, not the publisher. **M**
- [ ] 18. **Company pages**: "Every broker note on HDFC Bank, last 3 months". Headlines only — no ratings, targets or counts (user rule). Also good for Google. **M**
- [ ] 19. **Bookmark lists as links**: "My reading list this week". **S–M**
- [ ] 20. **WhatsApp / Telegram channel**: Big Stories twice a day, posted automatically. **S–M**
